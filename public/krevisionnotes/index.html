Chem SL · Paper 2[3 marks]
Calculate the pH of 0.020 mol dm⁻³ hydrochloric acid.
HCl is a strong acid so it dissociates fully: [H⁺] = 0.020 mol dm⁻³. pH = −log(0.020) = 1.7
3/3Markscheme marker
✓ Strong acid, full dissociation · ✓ [H⁺] = 0.020 · ✓ pH = 1.7 (2 s.f.)
✓ Full marks: every markscheme point matched
Physics SL · Paper 2[3 marks]
A 2.0 kg trolley accelerates from rest to 3.0 m s⁻¹ in 1.5 s. Calculate the resultant force.
a = Δv / Δt = 3.0 / 1.5 = 2.0 m s⁻². F = ma = 2.0 × 2.0 = 4.0 N
3/3Markscheme marker
✓ Acceleration = 2.0 m s⁻² · ✓ F = ma · ✓ F = 4.0 N
✓ Full marks: every markscheme point matched
