# User guide · 使用指南

## 1. Start

- **Pick your levels.** Click **SL/HL** at the top right (on phones: ☰ → *SL / HL levels*). Choose SL or HL for each subject. HL adds the AHL topics (marked **AHL**), HL papers such as Paper 3, and HL questions. You can switch at any time.
- **Sign in (optional).** Click **Sign in** and choose your Google account. Your progress is then saved online and appears on any device. Without signing in everything still works, but progress stays in that browser only. 唔登入都用得，不過進度只會留喺呢部機。

## 2. Learn: Notes

Open **Notes** → a subject → a topic. Each topic has:

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Formulas | Everything to recognise or use |
| Key concepts + **廣東話重點** | The ideas, with a Cantonese explanation in the purple boxes |
| Fastest methods · Traps | How to get the marks quickly, and where marks are usually lost |
| Worked examples | Full solutions in exam layout |
| **答題框架 · Answer frameworks** | Step-by-step structures for each question type (define, explain, calculate, evaluate, essays). Follow the steps in order to hit every markscheme point. |
| Key definitions | Learn these word-for-word for 2-mark "define" questions |
| Practice | Questions for the topic, sorted into sections |

**⬇ PDF notes** downloads the topic as a printable booklet. On the subject page, **PDF: all notes** downloads the whole subject. Click **Mark as revised** when you finish a topic (+10 XP).

## 3. Practise: Questions

- Filter by subject, topic, paper, type and difficulty. Type your answer and click **✓ Mark my answer**.
- The marker checks your answer strictly against the markscheme:
  - equivalent wording is accepted (OWTTE);
  - the direction must be right ("price rises" is not the same as "price falls");
  - common wrong answers are flagged and cost a mark;
  - in calculations, you get method marks for correct working, and units are checked (Physics and Chemistry);
  - essays are marked against the level descriptors, with a checklist: definitions, diagram, real example, evaluation, conclusion.
- It's a strict guide, not a real examiner. Always read the markscheme shown under your answer. If you disagree, use **Show markscheme & self-mark**.
- **⬇ IB-style PDF paper** turns the questions you have filtered into a printable exam paper: cover page, numbered questions with marks, lined answer boxes, and the markscheme at the back.

## 4. Test yourself: Quizzes & mocks

- **Unit quiz**: choose topics and a number of questions.
- **Smart quiz**: targets your weakest topics.
- **Mock exam paper**: timed, in the real paper structure. Markschemes are hidden until you submit, and you get a grade estimate at the end.
- **Calculation drill**: endless fresh numbers.
- **⬇ IB-style PDF** prints any quiz or mock to do on paper.

## 5. Fix mistakes: Mistakes notebook (錯題簿)

Every question where you lose marks is saved automatically, with your answer and the points you missed. Retry a question in place, or click **Retry all as a quiz**. Get full marks and the question moves to **Fixed** (+5 XP). **⬇ IB-style PDF of my mistakes** prints them as a paper.

## 6. Level up and compete

- **XP**: you get 1 XP per question answered, plus 2 XP per mark scored. Full marks on a written answer gives +3. A revised topic is +10, a quiz +15, a mock +40, a fixed mistake +5 and each completed daily quest +20.
- **Streak 🔥**: answer at least one question (or revise a topic) every day.
- **Daily quests**: 3 new quests every day, shown on the home page.
- **Badges**: see them all on **Progress**.
- **Friends**: sign in, open **Friends** and share your code or invite link. When a friend accepts, you both join a private **weekly league** (XP since Monday). Friends see your level, XP, streak, badges and subject progress. You can hide badges and subject progress. Friends **never** see your answers, essays, mistakes or past papers.

## 7. Your own past papers

**Papers** lets you add questions from official past papers you already have (from school or the IB store). They are kept private and join the bank, quizzes and marking.

## 8. Exam skills and IA/EE

- **Skills**: a framework for every question type in each subject, the markscheme codes explained, and practice with feedback.
- **IA & EE**: criteria in plain language, a self-assessment that predicts your mark and grade, a research-question checker, and the TOK/EE points table.

## Tips

- Dark mode: the ◐ button.
- Progress backup: **Progress → Export progress backup**. You don't need it if you're signed in.
- Everything here is original IB-style material, not official IB papers. Use it alongside the official past papers from your school.
